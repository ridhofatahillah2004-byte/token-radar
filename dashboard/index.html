How the value is worked out

Implied value = TVL × market multiple × growth factor × maturity factor × diversity factor. The market multiple is the median market-cap-per-dollar-of-TVL of the protocols in this snapshot that already record a token with a positive market cap (0.2227 here), so a launch is priced the way the market already prices tokens that trade. The growth factor measures the protocol’s seven-day TVL change against the snapshot week’s median (−8.2%), clamped to −50%…+100% (a missing change counts as 0%). The maturity factor is logarithmic in how long it has run with no token: 1 at listing, 1.5 at one year, 2.0 at three years (a missing listing date counts as the snapshot median age). The diversity factor discounts the second, third and later protocol of the same category by 5% a step, so the top of the board is not one category echoing itself. Only protocols that record no token are ranked, and wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade are left out. A protocol “records no token” in DefiLlama’s data; that is not proof it has none.
